GitHub shows what I built. It does not show what I am aiming at, and it says very little about how the pieces connect to each other.

That is the gap this site fills. The repositories stay the evidence; this is the index and the reasoning around them.

## What will be here

- **Project write-ups** — including the parts that failed, because that is usually where the useful information is
- **Paper and course notes** — written as "what I understood, what I still don't", not as summaries
- **Anything about training, evaluating or deploying a model that isn't obvious from the tutorial**

## What will not be here

Reposts of content I have not used, tutorials written from documentation alone, and progress claims without a link to something runnable.

## Why bother

Because the projects I want to do next — computer vision, medical imaging — are judged by people who will look for evidence before they look at a CV. A public record that accumulates slowly is more convincing than a polished page written the week before an application.

I will add posts when there is something real to say, which means this page will not update on a schedule.
